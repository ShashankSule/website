I participated in the super exciting Mathematical Research Community (MRC) on Explainable, Interpretable, and Adversarial AI at Beaver Hollow, NY!
A lot of things were discussed--including the capabilities of transformers, latent geometries of language models, and neural collapse. You can
hear more on these matters at the upcoming [Joint Math Meetings](http://jointmathematicsmeetings.org) in Seattle at the special session for this MRC.
