Two papers I've been working on are out. [One](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.18074) tells a story about how you can combine classical 
and deep learning methods for magnetic resonance imaging in the brain. [The other one](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.06689) 
shows how to use the Neumann eigenvectors of subgraphs for dimension reduction--with nearly isometric embeddings!
