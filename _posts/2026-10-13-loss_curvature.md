We identify curvature of a diffusion model's loss function as an important proxy for measuring two types of memorization in diffusion models:
(a) sample-level memorization that concerns an individual sample's propensity for being memorized, and (b) model-level memorization that 
describes whether a diffusion model's score structurally regurgitates the training data.
